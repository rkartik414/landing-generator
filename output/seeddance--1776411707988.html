AI Image Generation

AI Image Generation with Seedream 4.5

Seedream 4.5 is a high-performance multimodal image generation system designed to produce high-resolution, high-fidelity images from text prompts and visual inputs. The model unifies text-to-image synthesis, image editing, and multi-image composition within a single framework.

Accurately translates prompts into visuals with improved semantic understanding, helping teams turn creative direction into precise image outputs.

Generate native images up to 1K–4K resolution with strong visual fidelity for campaign assets, product visuals, and polished presentation-ready output.

Optimized for posters, ads, and text-heavy visual designs where readability and layout quality matter alongside creative aesthetics.

Combines multiple inputs while accurately maintaining subject consistency, useful for brand identity continuity and complex creative compositions.

Maintains composition, layout, and scene structure with high precision so outputs better match intended framing and art direction.

AI Video Generation

AI Video Generation with Seedance 1.5 Pro by Bytedance

Seedance 1.5 Pro is a next-generation generative model designed for native audio-visual generation, enabling synchronized creation of video and sound together. Built on a dual-branch diffusion transformer architecture, the model integrates cross-modal learning to produce coherent visual and audio outputs.

Create videos directly from text prompts, helping teams move from script or idea to visual motion concepts much faster.

Generate video and audio simultaneously with strong multimodal alignment for more coherent storytelling and more efficient production.

Supports multilingual and dialect-level lip synchronization, useful for localized content and region-specific campaigns.

Generate videos with dynamic camera movement and cinematic storytelling to achieve more dramatic, polished visual output.

Optimized inference pipeline significantly improves generation speed, reducing wait time for teams producing multiple variations.
